# Storage Reader Leases protect active downloads

Concurrent downloads hold durable, expiring Storage Reader Leases, including direct downloads whose lease lasts no longer than the signed URL. A renewable, fenced Merge Lease grants a single worker authority to complete the Merge; Part Reader Leases protect concurrent readers of Parts. Both expiring lease types renew every 30 seconds while active and expire after two minutes, except direct-download leases, which expire with their signed URLs. Parts are deleted only after the merge completes and no active Part Reader Leases remain; a Storage Location is deleted only after no Storage Reader Leases remain. A request that cannot acquire a Part Reader Lease because the merge has completed reloads the Storage Location and downloads its merged representation instead.
