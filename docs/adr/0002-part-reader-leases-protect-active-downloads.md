# Storage Reader Leases protect active downloads

Concurrent downloads hold durable, expiring Storage Reader Leases, including direct downloads whose lease lasts no longer than the signed URL. A renewable, fenced Merge Lease grants a single worker authority to complete the Merge; Part Reader Leases protect concurrent readers of Parts. Both expiring lease types renew every 30 seconds while active and expire after two minutes, except direct-download leases, which expire with their signed URLs. Parts are deleted only after the merge completes and no active Part Reader Leases remain; a Storage Location is deleted only after no Storage Reader Leases remain. A reader acquires its lease in the same locked transaction that reads the Storage Location's merge state, so its scope is chosen atomically: a download arriving before the merge takes a Part Reader Lease that blocks parts deletion, and one arriving after takes a Storage Reader Lease over the merged representation. There is therefore no window in which a reader can fail to acquire a Part Reader Lease against parts that are being deleted.
